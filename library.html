Library

An ongoing collection of books and films on art, design, photography, architecture, and culture. Borrowing is free.

How borrowing works
  • Browsing and borrowing are free.
  • Open to U.S. residents only.
  • Books are lent for 30 days and films for 7 to 14 days.
  • Austin pickup and returns are free.
  • If you’re outside Austin, you cover postage to receive your item. CAD sends a prepaid return label with it.
  • One renewal is available if nobody else has requested the item.
  • Please handle items with care. Lost or badly damaged items may need to be replaced.
