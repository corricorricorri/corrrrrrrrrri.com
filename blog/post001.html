01. GUIDE TO LIVING A NON-PERFORMATIVE LIFE Self-respect is possible. Plus photoshopping motion into your life, Weaving the dignity of others into your vocabulary, and other Profound thoughts on human worth in cursed times. - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -

Monday was a cold one here in Texas. Now if you know anything about this state, it’s that cold days are not only rare, but also an excuse to layer the fuck up. So, naturally, I dusted off an M65 jacket I'd been saving to pair with this vintage Ralph Lauren button-down I’d thrifted the day before. Granted, this thing had been washed wayyyyyyy too many times, but all those washes gave it this nice worn-in fade I really liked.

Plus, I’ve been rocking amekaji and ivy styles these days, so I took the opportunity to break in a pair of these full-grain leather Allen Edmonds I’d found for a steal at the Goodwill. But anyway, these are the kind of fits you show off right? So, I take a couple flicks, queue them up for Instagram and… hesitate.

Look at the sick-ass patina on this button-down -- can't beat classic Ralph amirite?

For a brief moment I was paralyzed by the idea of performance…or being a "performative male". It interrogated some deeper idea for me that I couldn’t readily verbalize. I posted the pictures anyway, because, hey, the fit was good. But, afterwards, I did something I never do — got open and personal onilne. Asking the same question I had just posed to myself, “As a man how do you post fits to the ram without being performative?”

Let’s be clear though, this idea or fear of being misunderstood isn’t new. Perception, by my definition is the innumerable number of alleys, byways and avenues within which you can be judged, categorized and otherwise humbled by your fellow man. To be perceived is to be known, to be seen. But what happens when people don’t like what they see? Not because you lack appeal (we know you’ve got taste if you’re here) but because you’re too cool.

I know you’re confused dear reader. You ask yourself, how could I not be universally adored online? My vibes are Immaculate. My fits are doing stupid numbers in the r/MFA subreddit as we speak, so what’s the issue?

Sorry bro, I regret to inform you that gone are the days that a man can get off a sick #OOTD without being labeled a veracious fuckboy, peacocking for women. But if you happen to find yourself among an ever-shrinking demographic of “chill dudes”, there may yet be hope for your evasion of this parasocial panopticon we call social media. Consider this your ever-growing guide to living a non-performative life.
