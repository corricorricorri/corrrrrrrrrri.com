Maintenance of Curiosity: Oana Stănescu

Corri Greene

Interview, originally published in 032c.

An interview with Oana Stănescu, an architect born in Romania and working in New York, about teaching, power and hierarchy in her field, collaboration, and getting ambitious projects built. Topics include her upbringing in Romania, working with Virgil Abloh, the + POOL project, and what it is like to be a woman in architecture.
