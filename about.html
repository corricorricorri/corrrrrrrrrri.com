April 6, 2026

Finding great art today is less about institutions and more about knowing where to look, when to pause, and how to recognize what resonates. The best gallery on earth happens to be the one in your pocket. That recognition stays with you long after you’ve scrolled past. I didn’t find most of my favorite work in galleries or classrooms, but through images shared online, often unexpectedly, usually on Instagram. A single post can stick with you in the same way, quietly shaping how you see and think. Having access to so much work, all the time, is something I don’t take lightly. Being intentional here means trusting your eye and engaging with what genuinely moves you. Read more »

March 27, 2026

I've been getting deeper into my curatorial practice lately — working as a fellow with the city and continuing to plan an exhibition for this summer. In my studies, I've become more interested in merchandise as a vehicle for conversation and engagement. Nothing excites me more than merch and ephemera. From an accessibility standpoint, you can't beat putting the work on someone’s back and letting that shit travel. I keep coming back to the role of the curator as a facilitator of dialogue within a community — not just presenting, but setting conditions for exchange and finding ways for the work to live beyond the gallery. I’ve put some of these thoughts down in writing. It’s all public. Take what’s useful and run with it. Download PDF »
