March 27, 2026

I've been getting deeper into my curatorial practice lately — working as a fellow with the city and continuing to plan an exhibition for this summer. In my studies, I've become more interested in merchandise as a vehicle for conversation and engagement. Nothing excites me more than merch and ephemera. From an accessibility standpoint, you can't beat putting the work on someone’s back and letting that shit travel. I keep coming back to the role of the curator as a facilitator of dialogue within a community — not just presenting, but setting conditions for exchange and finding ways for the work to live beyond the gallery. I’ve put some of these thoughts down in writing. It’s all public. Take what’s useful and run with it.

January 24, 2026

If you're reading this, it means I've given into the pressure of having a presence online. But this time I wanted to keep it simple. No images, no color, all text. In all honesty, I think I'm a bit tired of the whole, "look at my work" thing. I'm really an ideas guy at heart. like Nico said, I don't do too much talking these days. I'm dying of boredom in corporate America, so this is my Bat-Signal for the creative directors and suits to find me. The term multi-hyphenate gets thrown around a lot these days too, but I really do it all.
