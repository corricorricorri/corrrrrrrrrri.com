If you're reading this, it means I've given into the pressure of having a presence online. But this time I wanted to keep it simple. No images, no color, all text. In all honesty, I think I'm a bit tired of the whole, look at my work thing. I'm really an ideas guy at heart. like Nico said: I don't do too much talking these days. I'm dying of boredom in corporate America, so this is my Bat-Signal for the creative directors and suits to find me. The term multi-hyphenate gets thrown around a lot these days too, but I really do it all.
