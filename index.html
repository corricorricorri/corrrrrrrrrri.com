005. No Ghost, Just a Shell

Corri August 31, 2026

I have never been someone known for their consistency, as evidenced by my apparent lack of posts -- but hey, we’re here now. Lately, I’ve been going over tons of ideas in my head and what to do with them all. Is it better to dump them as IG stories, sniff around for some kind of publicized work, or just blog about it? I don’t consider myself one of those people with “posting anxiety” or whatever, but as I think of what to write about, I wind up grappling with the time-old question of why I want to tell anyone this in the first place.

And I think that question has become harder to answer because the internet has made every thought feel like it needs to become something. A thought can just be a thought. An observation can stay in your notes app. You can have an opinion and, incredibly, keep it to yourself. But once you put something online, it suddenly becomes content, and now we have to decide what kind of content it is. Is this a post? Is it an article? Is it a take? Is it a thread? Is it worth putting on Stories? Does it need a photo? Do I need to make a carousel? Should I wait until I have something more substantial to say? Read more »

002. Rental Family Is Very Important Movie Watching

Corri March 12, 2026

I watched Rental Family the other night with almost no expectations. Which, in my experience, is usually the best way to encounter a movie that ends up sticking with you longer than you planned. The premise is simple enough: In Japan, there are companies that allow you to rent people. Parents, partners, friends — to temporarily occupy roles in your life.

At first it sounds absurd. Maybe even a little bleak. But the more the film unfolds, the more it begins to feel like a strangely honest reflection of how people already live. Most of us are performing some version of ourselves anyway. Read more »

001. Guide to Living a Non-Performative Life

Corri February 24, 2026

Monday was a cold one here in Texas. Not just “light jacket” cold either — the kind that actually gives you permission to get dressed. And if you know this state, you know those days are exceedingly rare. Practically sacred. A legitimate excuse to layer the fuck up. I finally dug out this M65 jacket I’d been sitting on — waiting for the right moment to pair it with something that truly slaps — like this worn-in red Ralph Lauren button-down I’d thrifted just the day before.

The shirt had clearly been through the wash a few too many times. Wayyyyyy too many, if we’re being honest. But all that laundering gave it a perfect, broken-in fade. Lately I’ve been working a bit more amekaji and ivy style into the rotation, which felt like reason enough to finally break in this pair of full-grain leather Allen Edmonds I’d scored for basically nothing at the Goodwill last week.
